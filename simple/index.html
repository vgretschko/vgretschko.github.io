Working paper

Revise & resubmit · Journal of Political Economy

Stable Contracts under Renegotiation with Achim Wambach · PDF, October 2026

We study a principal contracting with a privately informed agent, where any contract is subject to renegotiation. Rather than specifying a renegotiation game, we propose conditions that the set of terminal outcomes of renegotiation must satisfy.
