Privacy Policy (Datenschutzerklärung)

I. The Short Version

This is the personal academic website of Vitali Gretschko. Unlike a commercial tech platform, I do not sell products here, I do not run analytics trackers, I do not use marketing cookies, and I have no plans to undergo a corporate merger or acquisition.

II. Data Collection & Local Storage

This website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics scripts. If you choose between the “Straight up” and “Fancy” views, or select a visual theme (Corten, Blackboard, Swiss, Terminal), your browser saves that preference locally on your own device (localStorage) so you don’t have to click it again next time. That information never leaves your browser and is never transmitted to me.

III. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This site is hosted as a static website on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you load a page, your browser inevitably transmits technical metadata (such as your IP address, browser type, and time of request) to GitHub’s servers so they can deliver the HTML file to your screen. This processing is technically necessary to display the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For details on how GitHub handles server logs, please refer to the GitHub Privacy Statement.

IV. External Links & Fonts

This site links to external academic repositories, publishers, and profiles (such as Google Scholar, SSRN, DOI links, and university pages). Once you click an external link, you leave my equilibrium path and are subject to the privacy policies of the respective destination site.

The Fancy view, this legal page, and the error page also load typefaces from Google Fonts. These font requests happen automatically when those pages load, before you click any external link, and transmit technical data such as your IP address to Google. For details, see Google Fonts privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

V. Contact & Your Rights

If you email me, I will use your email address and message solely to reply to your inquiry. Under the GDPR, you have the right to request access to, rectification of, or deletion of any personal data I hold about you. To exercise these rights, simply email vgretschko@web.de.
