I am an economic theorist working on auctions, mechanism design, and contracts. Because theorems occasionally misbehave when exposed to actual humans, I also test them in the laboratory and deploy them in real-world markets.

Since 2023, I have held the Chair of Sustainable Market Design at the University of Münster. Before returning to Westphalia, I was a tenured Professor of Market Design at the University of Mannheim and headed the Market Design research group at ZEW Mannheim, where we spent seven years turning game theory into public policy and corporate strategy.

My research asks a simple question: How do the rules of the game shape the behavior of the players? Specifically, I study when auctions beat face-to-face negotiations (almost always, if you’re the buyer), how to write contracts when everyone knows they will be renegotiated five minutes later, why smart people pay for useless information, and how to stop procurement managers from quietly favoring their golf buddies. These papers have found homes in Management Science, AEJ: Microeconomics, Theoretical Economics, the RAND Journal of Economics, and the Journal of Public Economics.

Outside the ivory tower, the math stays the same, though the stakes have more zeros. I have advised telecom bidders and regulators in multibillion-euro spectrum auctions across Europe, designed allocation mechanisms for food banks and municipal kindergarten spots, and helped major industrial firms rethink how they buy and sell. I originally trained as a mathematician in Münster before defecting to economics for my doctorate in Cologne, with research stops at Yale, FU Berlin, and Bonn.

Revise & resubmit at the Journal of Political Economy

Stable Contracts under Renegotiation

with Achim Wambach

What happens when a principal writes a contract with a privately informed agent, and both know they can tear it up and renegotiate tomorrow? Instead of sensitivity-testing a fragile, specific bargaining protocol, we define the axioms that any stable set of renegotiation outcomes must satisfy. This lets us bound the exact cost of lacking commitment—and shows the principal how to get the best possible deal when her hands refuse to stay tied.

Auctions vs. Negotiations: The Role of Communication

Procurement managers love to tell you that “talking to suppliers builds trust and lowers prices.” We brought experienced procurement professionals into a controlled experiment to check. Spoiler: communication systematically raises prices. If you want a friendly chat, go to a bar; if you want low prices, hold a silent auction.

Consulting & policy

For the past fifteen years, I have helped bidders, regulators, ministries, and corporations translate mechanism design into better rules and sharper bidding strategies. If you have an allocation problem, a high-stakes auction, or a procurement system that isn’t behaving, my inbox is open.

▚ Spectrum auctions

Advised telecom operators and regulators in high-stakes frequency auctions across Germany, Luxembourg, Poland, and Georgia, alongside an FCC filing on spectrum aggregation limits for the US incentive auction.

▚ Energy & climate

Advised bidders in Germany’s coal phase-out auctions and in UK and US offshore-wind lease auctions. Co-designed market rules for emergency gas storage and the 2030–2050 electricity market, because decarbonization is hard enough without bad auction formats.

▚ Industry & strategic procurement

Applied game theory, negotiation design, and B2B procurement architecture for BMW, Porsche, BOSCH, Mann + Hummel, Cimpress, and others—saving clients from the expensive habit of winging it.

▚ Public sector & social markets

Markets don’t always need money to clear. I helped design fair allocation mechanisms for food bank donations (Tafel Deutschland) and municipal kindergarten places, as well as public construction procurement (BBSR) and digital identity ecosystems (Federal Chancellery).

▚ Governments & international institutions

Optimized the bulk procurement of millions of textbooks for Brazil’s public school system, helped reform the Swedish Public Employment Service (with the OECD and EU DG REFORM), and advised the Georgian telecom regulator.

▚ Media & sports rights

Advised a major broadcaster in the 2016 Bundesliga TV rights auction—an environment where a single tweak to the tie-breaking or package rules can swing the price by hundreds of millions of euros.
